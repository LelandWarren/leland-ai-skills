# 5-Vector Deconstruction Engine

The 5-Vector Framework serves as the core taxonomy for dissecting and resolving complex, amorphous problem spaces. Every analysis must systematically run through these five vectors.

---

## Vector 1: Ontological Reality Filter (Rickover & Johnson)
* **Definition:** The foundational assumptions and spatial-analytical models through which the problem space is mapped, separating ground-truth physical/systemic realities from theoretical optimism ("paper models").
* **Deconstruction Criteria:**
  * **"Paper" vs. "Practical" Distinction:** Identify where theoretical models, vendor promises, or executive summaries obscure harsh operational realities.
  * **Unerasable Constraints:** Isolate non-negotiable physical, technical, mathematical, or financial limits that cannot be solved through policy or optimistic scheduling.
  * **Zero-Tolerance Risk Boundaries:** Identify failure modes where failure is catastrophic and unerasable, requiring zero-defect technical discipline.

---

## Vector 2: Topological Latency Audit (Skunk Works & Marshall)
* **Definition:** The structural mechanics, reporting pathways, authority flows, and feedback loops that govern decision velocity and operational friction.
* **Deconstruction Criteria:**
  * **Feedback Proximity (The 50-Foot Rule):** Calculate the physical, operational, or organizational distance between problem-identifiers (operators, technicians) and problem-solvers (architects, managers).
  * **Committee Gridlock & Latency:** Identify horizontal advisory bodies, multi-week approval processes, or proxy layers that delay decision-making.
  * **Vertical Command Alignment:** Map how to collapse horizontal coordination into direct vertical functional units with unmediated operational authority.

---

## Vector 3: Epistemic Stress-Testing (Von Neumann & Marshall)
* **Definition:** The explicit rules, mathematical models, and verification protocols used to evaluate truth claims, force trade-offs, and execute timely choices under uncertainty.
* **Deconstruction Criteria:**
  * **Decision Latency Cost:** Evaluate the systemic cost of delaying action versus the risk of rapid imperfect execution ("An imperfect act taken quickly is superior to a perfect act taken later").
  * **Game-Theoretic Trade-Off Mapping:** Formalize amorphous choices into explicit decision matrices, expected utility metrics, or game-theoretic options.
  * **Unvarnished Candor Mandate:** Eliminate consensus-seeking rhetoric; require explicit, transparent statements of technical trade-offs and resource bottlenecks.

---

## Vector 4: Anti-Empire Human Capital Alignment (Johnson & Rickover)
* **Definition:** The strategies used to select, deploy, and incentivize human capital, specifically guarding against administrative Parkinson's Law (work expanding to fill available headcount).
* **Deconstruction Criteria:**
  * **Hyper-Lean Team Scaling:** Restrict team sizes to 10%–25% of standard industry/organizational norms to force individual accountability and generalist agility.
  * **Incentive Decoupling:** Decouple compensation, titles, and advancement from headcount managed or budget size; tie rewards directly to engineering impact and problem resolution.
  * **Adversarial Stress Screening:** Evaluate technical leaders based on performance under stress, technical mastery of low-level mechanics, and psychological integrity.

---

## Vector 5: Parallel Sub-System Integration (Von Neumann & Johnson)
* **Definition:** The operational mechanics of translating high-level strategy into deployable hardware, software, or institutional structures under strict time and resource constraints.
* **Deconstruction Criteria:**
  * **Concurrent vs. Sequential Execution:** Identify linear dependencies (waiting for A to complete before starting B) and redesign them into concurrent sub-system development pipelines.
  * **Empirical & Computational Verification:** Replace slow physical trial-and-error with advanced simulation, or enforce early, continuous prototype testing to surface friction points immediately.
  * **Direct Quality Liability:** Shift quality ownership back to primary execution units and suppliers, eliminating redundant middle-tier inspection layers.
